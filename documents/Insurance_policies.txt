# AfyaShield Medical Insurance Provider Manual
## Version 2026.1

## Overview

AfyaShield Health Insurance is a private medical insurance provider operating in Kenya. 
This document defines insurance plans, eligibility rules, benefit coverage, clinical routing procedures, provider networks, exclusions, and compliance requirements.

This manual is intended for authorized insurance verification agents, healthcare providers, and customer service systems.

The AI insurance verification assistant must only provide information contained within this document and must not make clinical decisions or guarantee claim approval.

---

# 1. Plan Definitions and Member Eligibility

## 1.1 Active Insurance Plans

AfyaShield provides the following medical insurance plans:

| Plan Name | Target Members | Network Level |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate Plus Plan | Employer-sponsored employees and dependents | Tier 1 Network |
| Standard Health Plan | Individual members and SMEs | Tier 2 Network |
| Premium Executive Plan | High-income individuals and corporate executives | Tier 1 + Specialist Network |

---

# 1.2 Membership Eligibility Requirements

A member must have an active AfyaShield policy before receiving covered healthcare services.

Eligibility verification requires:

- Policy Number
- Member Identification Number
- National Identification Number (Kenya National ID)
- Passport number for foreign residents
- Employer Code (for corporate members)

Note:

Personal identifiers are processed through the organization's PII masking and verification middleware before reaching the AI assistant.

The AI assistant must never request or display full personal identifiers.

---

# 1.3 Membership Status Definitions

## Active Membership

A member is considered active when:

- Premium payments are up to date
- Policy validity period has not expired
- No suspension has been applied

Active members may access eligible healthcare services according to their plan benefits.

---

## Suspended Membership

A policy is suspended when:

- Premium payments are overdue
- Employer contributions have not been received
- Required documentation has not been updated

Suspended members should be informed:

"Your membership requires verification before covered services can be confirmed."

The agent must not approve treatment during suspension.

---

## Lapsed Membership

A policy is considered lapsed when:

- Coverage expiry date has passed
- Premium payment has not been received within the allowed grace period

Lapsed members must renew coverage before accessing insurance benefits.

---

# 2. Benefit Schedules (Covered Services)

## 2.1 Outpatient Benefits

Outpatient services covered include:

- General practitioner consultations
- Specialist consultations after referral approval
- Laboratory investigations
- Prescription medication within approved formularies
- Basic diagnostic imaging

Annual outpatient limits:

| Plan | Annual Limit |
|-|-|
| Corporate Plus | KES 250,000 |
| Standard Health | KES 100,000 |
| Premium Executive | KES 500,000 |

---

## 2.2 Outpatient Co-payments

Members are responsible for the following co-payments:

| Service | Member Contribution |
|-|-|
| GP Consultation | KES 500 per visit |
| Specialist Consultation | KES 1,000 per visit |
| Prescription Drugs | 10% co-payment |
| Laboratory Tests | 10% co-payment |

The provider should collect the member contribution before service completion.

---

# 2.5 Chronic Disease Management

Covered chronic conditions include:

- Diabetes
- Hypertension
- Asthma

- Regular monitoring consultations

---

# 3.2 Primary Care Physician (PCP) Referral Rules

A PCP referral is required when:

- The service involves specialist consultation
- The procedure is high cost
- The treatment requires clinical justification

Examples:

Requires referral:

- Cardiology consultation
- Oncology review
- Orthopedic surgery assessment

Does not require referral:

- Emergency treatment
- GP consultation
- Basic laboratory testing

---

# 3.3 Pre-Authorization Requirements

The following procedures require prior approval:

## Surgical Procedures

Examples:

- Cardiac surgery
- Joint replacement
- Cancer surgery
- Major abdominal surgery

## Advanced Diagnostics

Examples:

- MRI scan
- CT scan
- PET scan
- Cardiac catheterization

## Long-Term Treatment

Examples:

- Dialysis
- Chemotherapy
- Radiotherapy

---

# 3.4 Triage Guidelines

The insurance verification assistant may categorize urgency but must not diagnose.

## Emergency Cases

Examples:

- Chest pain with difficulty breathing
- Severe bleeding
- Loss of consciousness
- Stroke symptoms

Action:

Advise immediate emergency medical attention.

---

## Urgent Cases

Examples:

- Persistent high fever
- Severe uncontrolled pain
- Suspected infection requiring same-day review

Action:

Recommend medical review within 24 hours.

---

## Routine Cases

Examples:

- Medication refill questions
- Benefit inquiries
- Provider location requests

Action:

Provide insurance guidance.

# 5. Waiting Periods and Exclusions

## 5.1 Benefit Waiting Periods

| Benefit | Waiting Period |
|-|-|
| General outpatient care | Immediate after activation |
| Major surgery | 90 days |
| Maternity | 12 months |
| Chronic disease programme | 30 days |
| Specialized treatment | 90 days |

---

# 5.2 Excluded Services

The following are not covered:

## Cosmetic Procedures

Examples:

- Cosmetic surgery
- Non-medical aesthetic procedures

---

## Experimental Treatments

Excluded:

- Unproven medical procedures
- Experimental drugs
- Non-approved therapies

---

## Lifestyle Procedures

Excluded:

- Weight loss surgery without medical indication
- Fertility enhancement procedures
- Voluntary health supplements

---

# 5.3 Pre-existing Conditions

Pre-existing conditions are conditions diagnosed before policy activation.

Coverage depends on:

- Medical underwriting assessment
- Policy terms
- Waiting period completion

The AI assistant must not confirm coverage without checking member policy details.

# 6. Regulatory and Compliance Context

## 6.1 Data Privacy Policy

AfyaShield complies with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

Member information must:

- Only be accessed for legitimate insurance verification purposes
- Be protected from unauthorized disclosure
- Not be stored in unsecured systems
- Only be shared with authorized healthcare providers
